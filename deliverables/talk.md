# Talk reflection

Throughout the course, we will host a series of guest speakers sharing their work and insights. 
For each talk, you are expected to submit a brief reflection.

Your reflection should thoughtfully address the presentation. You might comment on aspects you found particularly interesting or thought-provoking (and why), highlight something new you learned, describe connections to other topics or your own experience, discuss possible applications or extensions of the ideas, or pose questions that emerged for you—along with any initial thoughts or speculations.

## Submission details
Reflections are due within 2 days of each speaker's presentation. Please submit your reflection via the Google Form [LINK TO BE UPDATED].

## Grading Criteria

Reflections will be evaluated based on timeliness of submission and the depth and thoughtfulness of your response. To receive full credit, your reflection should be sincere, demonstrate genuine engagement with the talk, and provide meaningful insights or questions. Points may be deducted for late submissions or for reflections that are superficial or lack substantive content.
